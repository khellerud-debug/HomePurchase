Annuity vs. Serial Loan

In Norway, there are two primary ways to structure your mortgage payments. The choice impacts your monthly cash flow and the total cost of the loan.

Annuitetslån (Annuity Loan)

How it works: You pay the exact same total amount every month (assuming the interest rate remains constant).

The catch: In the early years, your payment is mostly interest. You pay down the actual loan amount very slowly at first.

Why choose this? Predictable, lower initial monthly payments.

Serielån (Serial Loan)

How it works: You pay a fixed amount of principal plus decreasing interest.

The catch: Initial payments are significantly higher.

Why choose this? The total interest paid over time is much cheaper.
